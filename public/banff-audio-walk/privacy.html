Privacy

Review draft. The effective date will be set when this notice is published.

Location stays on your iPhone

When you tap Show my location, the app checks your position to show it on the Town of Banff map. When you start GPS audio for a walk or drive, your iPhone uses your location to play nearby stories, including while the screen is locked. The app processes that location on your iPhone. Manual listening works without location access. You can change access in iPhone Settings.

What the app saves

Your narrator choice, walking route progress and playback position are stored in the app on this iPhone. Offline audio is saved locally. Deleting the app removes its local copies.

Narration and downloads

The app plays recorded narration and can read stories using an installed iPhone voice. When you download narration, Cloudflare Pages serves the requested files from audio.banffbound.com. Cloudflare receives the file address, which identifies the route, voice and recording, and connection information such as your IP address. Your GPS coordinates and walking progress stay on the device.

Spires Digital Ltd does not keep a separate log of individual download requests. Cloudflare may retain connection data to deliver and protect its service. See the Cloudflare privacy policy.

Purchases and linked sites

Apple handles payment. The app uses StoreKit purchase and restore information to check access to the paid tour. When you open walking directions in Apple Maps, Apple Maps handles location and navigation. Other external links open under the destination’s privacy practices. Some activity links include a BanffBound affiliate identifier.

Contact

Spires Digital Ltd publishes Banff Audio Walk. For questions or to request deletion of information you shared with our support team, email support@banffbound.com.
