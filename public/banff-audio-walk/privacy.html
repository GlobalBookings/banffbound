Banff Audio Walk

Privacy

Effective November 7, 2026

Location and route requests

When you tap Show my location, the app checks your position to show it on the Town of Banff map. When you start GPS audio for a walk or drive, your iPhone uses your location to play nearby stories, including while the screen is locked. The app matches GPS stories on your iPhone. If you ask the app to calculate a driving route, Apple Maps processes the route request, including its start and destination. You can choose a named starting place instead of using your current position. Manual listening works without location access. You can turn off location access in iPhone Settings under Privacy & Security → Location Services → Banff Audio Walk.

What the app saves

Your narrator choice, walking route progress and playback position are stored in the app on this iPhone. Offline audio is saved locally. Deleting the app removes its local copies.

Narration and downloads

The app plays recorded narration and can read stories using an installed iPhone voice. When you download narration, Cloudflare Pages serves the requested files from audio.banffbound.com. Cloudflare receives the file address, which identifies the route, voice and recording, and connection information such as your IP address. Narrator download requests do not include GPS coordinates or walking progress.

Spires Digital Ltd does not use download requests to build a listening history or track visitors. Cloudflare may retain connection data to deliver and protect its service. See the Cloudflare privacy policy.

Purchases and linked sites

Apple handles payment. The app uses StoreKit purchase and restore information to check access to the paid tour. When you open directions in Apple Maps or Google Maps, that service handles location and navigation. Other external links open under the destination’s privacy practices. Some activity links include a BanffBound affiliate identifier.

Support and privacy requests

Spires Digital Ltd publishes Banff Audio Walk. If you contact support, we use your email address and the details you share to respond. We keep that correspondence only as long as needed to handle the request, maintain necessary business records or meet legal obligations.

For privacy questions or to ask us to access, correct or delete information you shared with our support team, email support@banffbound.com.
