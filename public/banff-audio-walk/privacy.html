Privacy

Review draft. Media host and retention details will be confirmed before release.

Location stays on your iPhone

When you start GPS audio for a walk or drive, your iPhone uses your location to play nearby stories, including while the screen is locked. The app processes that location on your iPhone. Manual listening works without location access. You can change access in iPhone Settings.

What the app saves

Your narrator choice, progress and playback position are stored in the app on this iPhone. Offline audio is saved locally. Deleting the app removes its local copies.

Narration and downloads

The app plays AI-generated recordings and can read preview stories using an installed iPhone voice. Downloading a voice sends a request to the audio host, which can receive standard connection information such as your IP address. Your GPS coordinates and walking progress stay on the device.

Purchases and linked sites

Apple handles payment. The app uses StoreKit purchase and restore information to check access to the paid tour. Links to external sites open those sites under their own privacy practices.
