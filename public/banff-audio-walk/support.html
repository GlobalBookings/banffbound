Banff Audio Walk

Support

For help with Banff Audio Walk, email support@banffbound.com.

Find a stop or read the tour

The six-stop Downtown Loop is free. Open Town of Banff to browse the offline map, choose a listening stop or find nearby cafés, restaurants and washrooms. Tap Find the railway station for the station and its audio stops. Use Show my location to see where you are.

Select a listening pin, then tap Read this stop or choose the text option for a specific walk at a shared pin. In the reader, use Text language to see the available translations. Transcripts and the town map work without location access. External links may need a connection.

Get audio ready before you leave

While connected, open your walk and tap Narrators & downloads. Choose Download or Download and use for your voice and wait for Ready offline. Play a story before leaving. If a download pauses, go back to that screen and choose the resume option. To switch to a voice already on the iPhone, tap its selection button, such as Use Marin for this tour.

Browse the drive map while parked

Open Cabin Radio and tap Browse drive map while parked with GPS audio off. The map works offline. Use Map area to explore a section or keep All areas selected to see all 96 GPS audio points. Tap a pin or a point in the list. When the story is available with your tour access and selected language, tap Read this story or Play this story.

Cabin Radio uses an installed iPhone voice. With the drive unlocked and the car parked, try Play voice sample with your iPhone offline before you set off. Follow road signs and use your usual navigation.

A story didn’t start

Try playing the selected stop manually. To use GPS audio, allow location while using the app and turn on Precise Location in iPhone Settings. For a walk, check Before you walk and tap Start GPS walk when available. If you miss a stop during a guided walk, tap Find this stop. Follow posted closures and detours.

Restore a tour purchase

Connect to the internet and use the Apple Account that bought the tour. On the Tours tab, open Purchases & restore and tap Restore purchases. Wait for the message confirming which tours are available. If no eligible purchase appears, check the Apple Account and try again. Apple manages purchase receipts and refunds.

Report a problem

Include the app version, iPhone model, iOS version, tour and stop name. A screenshot of the message is helpful. Keep passwords and payment details private.
