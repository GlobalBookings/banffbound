Support

For help with Banff Audio Walk, email support@banffbound.com.

Listen offline

The tour text and downtown map are bundled in the app. If you use an installed iPhone voice, check that your chosen voice is available before setting out. If a narrator has an offline download, wait until it shows Ready offline, then try playing a stop.

A story didn’t start

Open the player and play the current stop manually. To use GPS cues, allow precise location while using the app in iPhone Settings and start a guided walk. Before setting out, check current conditions and follow posted closures and detours.

Restore a tour purchase

Use the Apple Account that bought the tour. Open Walk or Drive, tap Unlock tours or restore purchases, then tap Restore purchases while connected. For payment records or a refund, use your Apple Account purchase history.

Report a problem

Include the app version, iPhone model, iOS version, tour and stop name. A screenshot of the message is helpful. Keep passwords and payment details private.
