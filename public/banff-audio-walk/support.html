Banff Audio Walk

Support

For help with Banff Audio Walk, email support@banffbound.com.

Listen offline

The walking transcripts and maps are available offline. For recorded narration, open your walk and tap Narrators & downloads. Tap Download or Download and use for the voice you want and wait for Ready offline. To switch to a voice already on your iPhone, tap its selection button, such as Use Marin for this tour. Play a stop before you leave. If the download pauses, return to that section to resume it.

A story didn’t start

Open the player and play the current stop manually. For GPS audio, allow Precise Location while using the app in iPhone Settings, then start a GPS walk. If you miss a stop, use Find this stop during the walk. Check current conditions before setting out and follow posted closures and detours.

Restore a tour purchase

Use the Apple Account that bought the tour. Connect to the internet, open Purchases & restore from Home, and tap Restore purchases. Apple manages purchase receipts and refund requests.

Report a problem

Include the app version, iPhone model, iOS version, tour and stop name. A screenshot of the message is helpful. Keep passwords and payment details private.
