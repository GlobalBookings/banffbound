Help with your BanffBound trip

Contact BanffBound for app help or a content correction.

Before you leave connectivity

Open your trip and finish its offline-save action while connected. Reopen it once with connectivity off to check the details you need. In the browser, wait for the completed-save message. Online maps, external source pages, weather updates and booking services are separate and need connectivity.

Your saved trip

Plans are stored on this device or browser. They do not automatically appear on another device. Clearing app or site data can remove them. Share or export a copy before resetting or changing devices.

If a download fails

Reconnect, check available storage and retry. Keep the last complete version until the new download finishes. The app should report completion only after its files have been verified. Browser storage may be cleared by the browser or operating system.

Booking changes

Booking checklist entries and notes are your own records. A marked checklist item is not provider confirmation. For tickets, payment, cancellations or refunds, contact the operator or provider you booked with.

Report a problem

Tell us your device, operating system or browser, the app version if shown, the screen and what happened. Avoid sending passwords, payment details or private reservation documents. Content corrections are welcome; include the place or guide title and a source link if available.
