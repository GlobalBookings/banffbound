## Family guide: material corrections

- Replaced age-to-hike-distance prescriptions with a filterable planning shortlist. Age bands organise ideas; they do not establish ability, supervision, operator admission or a route's suitability.
- Removed fixed excursion prices, assumed free child admissions, universal free cots/high chairs and invented personal-experience claims. Tickets, room allocation and product conditions must be checked for the actual travellers.
- Corrected Banff Gondola stroller/mobility distinctions and Lake Minnewanka cruise boarding access. A venue with accessible facilities does not establish an accessible outdoor trail or boardwalk.
- Corrected Norquay tubing and via-ferrata eligibility. The latter is a guided summer activity, not a general winter outing for children aged eight.
- Removed the invented Banff recreation-centre pool and replaced it with The Fenlands' actual skating/recreation role and Elevation Place's session-specific swim option in Canmore.
- Replaced unsupported blanket family-rafting ages, purported canoe access to Paint Pots, an unverified Banff Dark Sky Preserve claim and automatic alpine-hike recommendations with researched products and specific checks.
- Bow Falls is no longer described as a completely flat, paved, year-round stroller path. Johnston Canyon is not presented as currently open: the page flags the dated closure listed in the current Parks bulletin index and asks visitors to verify reopening.

## Dog guide: material corrections

- Corrected the false claim that no off-leash areas exist in Banff. Designated Town areas are distinct from national-park trails and have current posted rules and closures.
- Used Parks Canada's leash/physical-control wording rather than an unsupported universal numerical leash length. Removed the misleading automatic fine range.
- Removed invented permanent dog bans for all Lake Agnes, Plain of Six Glaciers, Moraine Rockpile, Consolation Lakes and most backcountry trails. The guide requires exact route/date checks; it does not replace those false bans with universal permission.
- Added the actual seasonal Lake Minnewanka dog restriction and distinguished it from the cruise operator's discretionary pet acceptance.
- Corrected public-transport eligibility: an ordinary leashed dog is not automatically accepted; lap-sized animal-carrier rules apply. Service-animal arrangements are treated separately without copying a universal certification rule.
- Removed claims that dogs can be left while the party enters an attraction, can drink lake water or can swim in every lake, along with unverified lake temperatures and route descriptions. The page makes supervision and current restrictions part of the plan.
- Retained only a primary-source-backed patio and published pet-room options. Removed universal patio acceptance, invented pet fees/weight limits and assumptions that a pet room permits restaurant, pool or attraction access.
