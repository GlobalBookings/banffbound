# Finite 81-record trail source audit — 2026-10-29

## Scope and result

The public `trails` export contains the same 81 IDs/slugs, indicative coordinates and inherited image URLs. This pass source-reviews the 58 remaining summer/year-round hiking records against the current detailed Parks Canada profiles. The 23 previously reviewed records retain their valid figures, prose, access notices, evidence and review dates; their review scopes are made explicit. One necessary correction to those prior records is **Ink Pots: Easy → Moderate**, matching the current Parks classification; its other facts and date are unchanged.

**Final inventory: 80 source-reviewed route listings, 1 retained unverified winter legacy URL.** There are 80 directory-eligible entries. `golf-course-loop-xc-ski` stays excluded from the directory and noindex on its retained detail URL. No new unverified hiking variant was substituted for a nearby route. Dates are editorial source-check dates, not trail-condition timestamps.

All 58 new reviews are expressly **summer hiking descriptions**, including former year-round labels. A summer grade is not evidence of winter suitability. The 23 retained scopes distinguish prior summer, multiple-season and winter activity reviews. No field supplies live opening, snow, grooming, ice, parking, crowd or wildlife-sighting information.

## Measurement decisions and material corrections

- **Detailed profile preferred where the same publisher conflicts with itself:** Cory Pass detailed **11.7 km loop** versus summary **13 km one way**; Sunshine detailed **10.3 km network** versus summary **10.2 km**; Mistaya detailed **0.6 km one way** versus summary **0.5 km**. These differences are visible in route prose, not silently reconciled into invented precision. Nigel Pass detailed return estimate is 4–5 hours; overview rounds to 5.
- **Endpoint and direction:** Twin Lakes uses the exact **Lower Twin Lake via Arnica** profile, passing Upper Twin on the way, not an invented upper-lake distance. Sheol/Paradise is **3.9 km connector only**, 1–2 hours one way; approaches/exits excluded, outward elevation specifically Saddleback towards Paradise. The two Sentinel approaches are distinct (14.2 km Paradise versus 5.6 km Larch, each source one way); the unusually long Paradise profile/time is explicitly attributed, never used to derive a pace or safe start time.
- **Network/loop/approach:** Sunshine figures are a network overview, not one compulsory lift-assisted loop. Sundance is a **3.7 km one-way approach + 2.1 km hiking-only canyon loop**, about 9.5 km for the complete walk, and Moderate. Bow River Lake Louise is **up to 5.7 km one way**, not a complete loop. Fairview Lookout is distinct from the mountain; Cascade Amphitheatre is distinct from Cascade summit scrambling.
- **Current detailed figures:** Tunnel Campground 6.3 km loop/110 m; Upper Stoney 4.4 km loop/210 m; Cascade Amphitheatre 6.8 km one way/740 m gain/200 m loss; Bourgeau 6.7 km one way/730 m gain; Harvey 8.9 km total one way from the same start/1,010 m gain. Do not add their full totals twice. All other reviewed numerical profiles are recorded below.
- **Missing is not zero:** unreported descent is explicitly not stated. Vista has published 110 m outward loss but no published outward gain; Moraine shoreline gives “minimal”, not a measured 0. Taylor Lake explicitly publishes 0 m outward loss; this isolated zero is retained and does not remove the return descent.
- **Geography:** Boom Lake is on Highway 93 South **in Banff National Park, Alberta**; Stanley Glacier remains Kootenay National Park, British Columbia. Banff Lake Annette is Paradise Valley at Mount Temple, not Jasper’s lake. Healy Pass starts at the Sunshine **base**, not at the lift-served upper Meadows network. Hidden/Deception start at **Fish Creek**, with the access-road walk included, not at a lift top.
- **Season/access:** current lower Tramline alignment and Bow River west-bank closures are separated from the rest of each route (May 15–October 15). The current grouped Lake Louise Moderate/Difficult profile avalanche warnings are reflected for the newly reviewed affected routes, with the published Deception/Tramline exceptions never treated as winter safety approvals. Minnewanka annual restriction boundaries are explicit.
- **Grade:** retain the internal `challenging` enum for compatibility, but newly reviewed profiles also store the actual publisher grade `difficult`, used on details. Sundance, Taylor Lake and Ink Pots are Moderate in current official descriptions. Easy is never turned into paved, accessible, universally suitable or winter-safe.

No shared metadata, sitemap, CI or map-behaviour changes are included.
