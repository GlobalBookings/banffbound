# Winter library quality and finite content inventory — 2026-10-28

## Inventory boundary and method

Read-only inventory taken from the deployed-main-based feature branch before this module refresh. The actual blog rendering merges blogContent.ts, blogContent3.ts and the reviewed overrides; allBlogPosts generates 335 records, of which 40 are redirect aliases and 295 are unique canonical posts. The table below includes every canonical slug exactly once. All 295 have a rendered body; four have fewer than 350 plain-text words. Existing source-backed overrides cover 32 canonicals (31 winter articles plus banff-view). The other 263 are not declared accurate. 78 unreviewed canonical bodies match injected/unverified first-person patterns.

This is a complete inventory and content screening, not a completed factual audit of the legacy library. Prior reviewed status means an existing bounded source record/override, not timeless accuracy. Word counts exclude HTML tags; a pattern flag is a review lead rather than proof of a particular fact. P0 means priority safety/access/regulatory or explicit current-event risk; P1 seasonal/route/current-source review; P2 commercial/local logistics or intent overlap; P3 remaining general/historical review. Every unreviewed row retains that status.

Selected distinct canonical refreshes: banff-avalanche-safety (visitor decision-making and education); banff-park-bus (which network/ticket serves the journey); banff-shoulder-season (May/October tradeoffs); banff-new-years-eve (2026/27 planning, no invented fireworks); banff-weather-best-time-to-visit (weather interpretation/packing rather than a second general best-time ranking). Root owns shared body/metadata wiring. No canonical URL or redirect is changed here.

## Highest-priority remaining findings

- Johnston spelling variants and the general Johnston/Ink Pots guides still use open/access language despite the current main-trail/P1 closure. Assess consolidation to a maintained destination guide rather than preserving four competing same-intent pages.
- Arrival blog variants repeat the wrong Highway 1 tire law, obsolete On-It options and even Route 9 as Calgary transit. The reviewed /calgary-to-banff and /car-free-banff landings are the maintained journey; decide their relationship to the blog aliases.
- Accessibility trail lists need route-specific source review; a generic easy hike is not proof of wheelchair suitability.
- Several Jasper road-trip, Bow Summit/Peyto, Bow Lake, Num-Ti-Jah, Sandman, restaurant, nightlife and budget/fee pages compete for substantially overlapping intent. Group membership is a consolidation candidate, not an instruction to redirect without checking content/search history.
- Unreviewed permit, work-authorisation, accommodation-regulation and price claims require the relevant primary sources; no current legal/price accuracy is inferred from a 2026 title.
