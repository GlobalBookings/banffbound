# Trip cost planner review — 29 October 2026

Scope: `src/pages/trip-cost.astro`, new `src/data/tripCostPlanning.ts`, new `src/components/TripCostInteractions.astro` and focused `scripts/check-trip-cost-planning.mjs`. This review does not update other articles or promise prices for any date, room, traveller or product.

## Changes and factual boundary

The previous calculator supplied unsupported 2026 hotel, food, activity, transport and pass amounts; fixed advance-booking/seasonal savings; and recommendations tied to fabricated budget tiers. Its hostel bed/hotel room assumptions shared one multiplier, lodging used travel days as nights, the transport description included a park-pass portion while admission was also added, and the static examples did not match the calculated totals. Without JavaScript, its option lists and breakdown were empty and its result showed $0.

The replacement has no market-price defaults, fixed discounts, FX table or price/availability API. People, total travel days, accommodation nights and room/unit count are separate. The same trip shape is used for Plan A and optional Plan B. Users supply CAD amounts including their applicable taxes and required fees. The interface asks them to use their actual dates/party and check their package inclusions; it does not infer ticket eligibility from party size.

Seven cost rows are accommodation, food/drinks, activities/equipment, getting around, getting here/home, admission/permits, and other trip costs. Accommodation offers per-room/night, per-person/night (same bed rate for each person), whole-party/night or whole-party/trip. Food offers per-person/day, whole-party/day or whole-party/trip. The other rows take a complete whole-party trip amount so different ages, rental durations and included products are not silently multiplied by the wrong quantity.

A user can enter an amount, explicitly mark no extra cost/already counted elsewhere, or deliberately omit a category. Blank is unknown. Zero is valid when entered or explicitly included. Omissions and unknowns produce only a clearly marked partial budget and never a whole-trip comparison. Nightly accommodation contributes zero for zero nights, without requiring a made-up room quote. A one-off accommodation amount still counts for a zero-night trip if entered. The optional reserve is the user's percentage of entered costs only; it does not price unknown costs or predict extra charges.

Known amounts are accumulated in integer cents, with the percentage reserve rounded once to cents. Per-person and per-person/day results are equal planning splits, rounded for display; they do not claim each traveller owes the same amount. Both complete plans plus the currency/tax/inclusion confirmation are required to compare totals. A lower entered total is not called a market saving, recommended product or best deal. Already-paid amounts should remain in a total-trip budget.

## Current primary source routes

Opened directly on 29 October 2026; none of their operational amounts is copied into defaults, prose examples, structured data or the client model:

- [Parks Canada — Banff fees](https://parks.canada.ca/pn-np/ab/banff/visit/tarifs-fees): official admission/pass/permit and other applicable fee categories. The site directs users to check the appropriate party, ages, vehicle and existing pass instead of multiplying a universal daily admission amount by everyone.
- [Roam Transit — fares](https://roamtransit.com/fares/): official local/regional route-specific fares and passes. A generic transit-per-day price does not establish the journeys the party needs. The planner takes the user's whole-trip getting-around total.
- [SkiBig3 — lift tickets](https://www.skibig3.com/ski/lift-tickets/): current ticket product/booking information with date, age and product distinctions. The older `/lift-tickets/` URL redirected here during this review, so the page uses this current direct route. The planner does not assume lessons, rental equipment, lift access or travel are universally included.

Internal quote and decision handoffs use existing `/book/banff-hotels/`, `/book/lake-louise-stays/`, `/book/banff-winter-activities/`, `/eat-and-drink/`, `/winter-stays`, `/ski-pass-calculator`, `/calgary-to-banff` and `/car-free-banff`. The original `/hotels` and `/trip-builder` destinations remain. There were no native external affiliate links in the old calculator to replace; the new page constructs no merchant URL and does not alter downstream affiliate identifiers. A verifier review caught and corrected a draft `/book/winter-activities/` path to the actual `/book/banff-winter-activities/` route before release.

## Progressive and privacy contract

The complete manual worksheet is server-rendered, visible and printable without JavaScript. It contains seven cost rows plus subtotal/reserve/total formulas and Plan A/B columns. The interactive form stays hidden until all required nodes initialize successfully. All inputs have associated labels; result tables have row/column headings; the comparison uses a polite live status, including the entered Plan A total when comparing only one plan. Errors are text-only, marked on the relevant fields and focused after submit. Editing removes stale result numbers until Calculate is used again. Native reset restores party2/days5/nights4/rooms1/reserve0 and blank quotes, hides Plan B and clears errors. Print is an explicit user action.

There is no storage, fetch, cookie access, address-bar parsing, quote-sharing URL, clipboard, HTML injection or merchant-link mutation in the controller. The form has autocomplete off and no field names that could be submitted as a fallback query. Browser behaviour is not described as a guaranteed reset or erasure mechanism. Clear & start again resets the calculator's current controls; the application does not save them. A partial initialization/render failure removes installed handlers, hides interactive controls and leaves the worksheet available. Currency is fixed to CAD; no foreign-exchange rate is invented.
