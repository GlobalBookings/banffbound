## Official safety and seasonal sources

- Parks Canada, [Keep the wild in wildlife / viewing space](https://parks.canada.ca/pn-np/ab/banff/visit/faune-wildlife/espace-space): minimum100m bears/wolves/cougars/coyotes;30m deer/elk/sheep/moose; animal behaviour means increase distance; no approach/feed/entice/follow/crowd; roadside stay-in-vehicle, safe/legal brief stops and move on from jams. No numeric rule invented for a species absent from that list.
- Parks Canada, [Safe travel in bear country](https://parks.canada.ca/pn-np/mtn/ours-bears/securite-safety/ours-humains-bears-people): unaware versus aware; defensive versus non-defensive/predatory; calm retreat/no running; defensive contact play-dead posture; ongoing/predatory attack fight back. Species/colour alone is not the response decision. Voice/group/food/dogs preparation; bells insufficient. Public guide links the full instructions; no emergency behavioural calculator.
- Parks Canada, [Elk safety](https://parks.canada.ca/pn-np/ab/banff/securite-safety/faunesecurite-wildlifesafety/wapiti-elk):30m; town elk are wild; nervous/ears-back/teeth-grinding means back off; no passage between cow/calf or a group; temporary calving closures. Do not recycle unsupported legacy elk-charge or knocked-down instructions.
- Parks Canada, [Living with wildlife policy appendix](https://parks.canada.ca/agence-agency/bib-lib/politiques-policies/science/nature/nature04): general elk calving mid-May–endJune, mating mid-Sep–endOct. **Source conflict:** Banff’s older elk page says Aug–Sep, while the [2026 rut advisory](https://parks.canada.ca/pn-np/ab/banff/bulletins/868a8410-7ef6-4f87-a4d3-f8dc44f335aa) body says until mid-Oct but its end date is31Oct2026. Therefore use fall/September–October caution, disclose variation in calendar FAQ, no predicted peak/end or safe-after date. Never separate bull/females or place a vehicle between them.
- Parks Canada, [Carnivore safety](https://parks.canada.ca/pn-np/ab/banff/securite-safety/faunesecurite-wildlifesafety/securite-carnivores-safety): approached by fearless wolf/coyote/cougar, stand ground, act aggressively, pick up children, no running/jumping towards animal; routine reporting1470.
- Alberta, [Cougars and outdoor recreation](https://www.alberta.ca/cougars-and-outdoor-recreation): no running/play-dead; make large, keep children close; fight back if attacked, get back up, target face/eyes. No nocturnal-only/rare-sighting reassurance.
- Parks Canada, [Banff safety](https://parks.canada.ca/pn-np/ab/banff/securite-safety):911 emergency; limited cell coverage; wildlife reporting403-762-1470 with description/behaviour/location,24/7. [Banff winter safety](https://parks.canada.ca/pn-np/ab/banff/activ/skidefond-xcski/banff) separately lists403-762-4506 for satellite-phone emergency, winter bear-spray packing, bears waking briefly, other wildlife active, no following tracks. Source dates are not observation times.
- Parks Canada, [Bow Valley Parkway](https://parks.canada.ca/pn-np/ab/banff/visit/les10-top10/promenade-de-la-vallee-de-la-bow-bow-valley-parkway): annual1March–25June,8p.m.–8a.m.,17km Johnston Canyon Campground–Fireside Picnic Area; all travel including cycling/walking. Not the entire Parkway; do not confuse overnight habitat restriction with separate seasonal vehicle restrictions. [Current bulletins](https://parks.canada.ca/pn-np/ab/banff/bulletins) and signs remain the date-specific access source.

## Bear-spray rules, training, transport and disposal

- Parks Canada, [Bear spray](https://parks.canada.ca/pn-np/mtn/ours-bears/securite-safety/gaz-spray): exact label/expiry, accessible holster, wind/rain/cold effects, transport clip/trunk/sealed container; no spraying body/tent/equipment. Body/video contain differing example range/burst details; do not turn an example into a universal canister instruction.
- Alberta, [Deterrents — bear spray and noisemakers](https://www.alberta.ca/deterrents-bear-spray-and-noisemakers): exact canister label range/duration, compatible holster, inert practice, training, avoid extreme vehicle temperatures, transport container, no commercial airlines. Supports no fixed expiry/range/time or success-percentage claim. Alberta material about separate noisemakers is not blanket permission for pyrotechnics in Banff National Park; these pages do not recommend them.
- CATSA, [Outdoor adventure](https://www.catsa-acsta.gc.ca/en/travel-tips/outdoor-adventure): bear spray prohibited in both carry-on/checked baggage. Local purchase/rental then return/disposal before departure. [Transport Canada dangerous-goods FAQ](https://tc.canada.ca/en/dangerous-goods/safety-awareness-materials-faq): aircraft survival-kit exceptions are not generic passenger permission; ask specialty operators in advance.
- CBSA, [D19-13-2 weapons classification](https://www.cbsa-asfc.gc.ca/publications/dm-md/d19/d19-13-2-eng.html): animal-use label/design and Pest Control Products Act requirements distinguish animal repellents from prohibited human-use sprays; no minimumquantity/capacity test for animal-repellent classification. Do not claim a universal500ml legality rule or personalised import clearance. No purchase-age conclusion inferred from unrelated rental terms.
- Roam, [Transportation policies](https://roamtransit.com/about-us/transportation-policies/): properly sealed, safety cap intact, inside passenger bag/backpack throughout ride; not hand/seat/open carry. Change to accessible trail carry after leaving bus. This policy cannot be transferred to other shuttle/coach operators.
- Town of Banff, [Transfer Site](https://banff.ca/1266/Transfer-Site):160HawkAvenue; accepts household hazardous waste; Operations403-762-1240; non-residential/out-of-town terms differ. Public page asks callers to confirm bear-spray canister acceptance, visitor terms and current hours, with no free-disposal or automatic acceptance promise. Also inspected [Annual Recycling Roundup](https://banff.ca/549/Annual-Recycling-Roundup), but event details are **past2025 and residents-only**, so do not promote that event as a visitor solution. [Calgary official disposal](https://www.calgary.ca/waste/what-goes-where/chemicals-bear-spray.html): empty/full cans hazardous-waste hand-in, do not puncture/crush or put in ordinary carts. No claim every fire station accepts them.
- Health Canada, [POISON-X announcement](https://www.canada.ca/en/health-canada/news/2023/03/canada-launches-new-toll-free-1-844-poison-x-number-for-poison-centres.html):1-844-764-7669 routes to poison advice; no homemade treatment protocol, harmlessness or recovery-duration claim.911 for emergency.
