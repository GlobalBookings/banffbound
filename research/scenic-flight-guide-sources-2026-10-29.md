## Replaced claims and visitor value

The guides remove invented route categories, unverified helicopter-company identities, old fare bands, March2026 promotion/copy, universal weight/age/refund/advance-booking rules, precise road/flight/queue timing, a presumed Kananaskis Village heliport, Cline River mislocated in Kananaskis, medication timing, motion/smoothness assurances, camera-seat promises and made-up personal experience. No animal sighting, cloud-free view or winter flight/landing is promised.

The replacement is more than caution copy: the general guide compares three experience formats and three actual base journeys, explains the specific difference between helicopter boarding and reaching the facility, and supplies a complete five-part enquiry brief. The Kananaskis guide starts with the real address, names verified catalogue examples, explains direct-enquiry landing products and works through approach, transfer, briefing, return and the rest of the day. Tables, sources, all content and FAQs are fully server rendered; a custom article-body script would add little to these decisions, so none is inserted.

Native external links are direct primary operator/source routes, without `partner_id`, `camref`, a guessed product, automatic redirect or vendor loader. Internal accommodation comparison links are explicitly separate from flight bookings. Existing external affiliate identifiers elsewhere in the site remain untouched. Related current journeys include the reviewed `/blog/banff-heli-skiing/` and `/blog/banff-ski-touring/`, `/towns/canmore/`, `/towns/banff/`, `/car-free-banff`, `/winter-stays`, `/winter`, `/weather`, `/what-to-do-today` and the existing `/book/banff-hotels/` comparison.
