## URL, privacy and progressive enhancement

Address keys are exactly `heli_operator`, `heli_base`, `heli_transport`, `heli_overnight`, `heli_equipment`. Each must occur once and match an enum. Unknown/duplicate values reset that dimension, produce a plain visible note and are removed/normalised on the next address update. Other address-bar parameters and its hash remain intact locally. No unknown value is rendered, echoed into provider links or concatenated into an enquiry.

**Copied settings URLs are different from the preserved local address:** `buildHeliSkiShareUrl` creates the fixed public canonical `https://banffbound.com/blog/banff-heli-skiing/`, only non-default allowlisted choices, and `#heli-ski-planner`. It discards unrelated UTM/click IDs, arbitrary query values, private reader inputs, preview hostname and other fragments. The enquiry outline contains public enum labels and fixed source-based questions only. Users add the real date/party/experience themselves through the operator’s official process. Copying either format does not contact an operator, reserve a place or validate a transfer. The controller has no network, storage, cookies, HTML injection, provider URL rewriting or automatic navigation.

Both cards, the complete operator sources, default journey questions, three booking checks and bounded cat comparison are SSR. The five-option form stays hidden without JavaScript, and the FAQ export is fully readable without it. Initialisation fails back to that full content if required DOM/card identities/fields are absent. Repeated Astro page-load events do not duplicate handlers. Controls change card visibility/text without reparenting the cards or their links. Clipboard failure reveals a labelled readonly textarea, intentionally focuses/selects the public text, and reports that nothing was sent. Reset clears only planner choices and restores defaults; blocked browser history does not prevent updates or copying current settings.
