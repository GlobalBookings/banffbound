Distinct purpose: decide between a town visit, a dedicated activity and a Golden overnight; join the actual accommodation/road/transport/meeting-point/return chain. This does not duplicate the reviewed specialist heli-ski article or pretend the Banff three-resort ski planner includes Kicking Horse.

## Material inherited claims replaced

- Fixed `135 km / 1.5 hours`, an unqualified “90 minutes west”, and a clock itinerary arriving at 9:30am and returning to Banff by 7pm are removed. A route description is not an arrival or winter feasibility guarantee.
- The forced gondola + summit hike + afternoon rafting + “quick” Takakkaw Falls return is removed. Activity check-in and final transport are preserved in separate suggested formats, without made-up elapsed times.
- The refuge is not described as multiple bears at the base; the operator's actual Boo/refuge programme is linked without inventing its current tour access or guaranteeing a sighting.
- Universal half-day family/novice rafting claims and a full-day lower-canyon guarantee are removed. The selected operator/trip, section, eligibility and river-base location must be checked.
- No unsupervised wetlands paddling route or winter natural-ice route is recommended. A land-based town outing is an option subject to actual surface and daylight, not a winter-maintenance claim.
- Golden town, the resort, Dawn Mountain and CMH Purcell have separate arrival requirements. A heli-ski day is not forced into a same-day resort visit or Banff return. The CMH day lodge is not an overnight lodge.
- Generic Banff affiliate searches are replaced with relevant internal Golden/base and specialist planning handoffs. No provider offer, property ID, saving, price, availability or new affiliate relationship is invented. No affiliate identifier is changed in an existing file.
