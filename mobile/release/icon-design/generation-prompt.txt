Use case: logo-brand
Asset type: final mobile app icon artwork, 1024 by 1024 square.
Primary request: Create a premium, distinctive and immediately readable icon for an independent Banff mountain travel companion. A bold white snow-capped Canadian Rockies massif above luminous turquoise Lake Louise water, on a deep forest-green #163b31 background, with one small warm gold sun accent. Preserve the restrained forest-green and warm-gold feel of the existing brand while making the result more inviting and polished.
Style: exceptionally clean contemporary editorial illustration, vector-like crisp silhouettes and a few broad sculpted color facets; intentional simple geometry, elegant proportions, premium outdoor travel identity. This is an app icon, not a scenic photograph or a poster.
Composition: a single compact central alpine-and-lake motif occupying roughly the middle 70 percent of the square; one dominant sharp white peak with a smaller companion ridge, a confident sweep of bright turquoise lake in the lower foreground, and a clean gold sun nestled above one shoulder of the mountain. Strong readable white/turquoise contrast against dark green. Clear negative space separating the mountain, lake and sun; no miniature details, trees or buildings. The motif must remain recognizable at 60 pixels and survive the normal iOS and Android circular icon masks.
Color palette: deep forest green #163b31 to the edges, warm off-white snow, cool teal shadow facets, bright glacial turquoise water, restrained warm gold sun.
Constraints: full-bleed square with all corners completely filled with the dark forest-green background. Opaque RGB appearance. No text, letters, monogram, words, logos, watermark, border, frame, badge, app mockup, device, white margins, or baked-in rounded corners. No glossy plastic, fussy texture, photorealistic scenery or tiny linework. Deliver one finished strong concept, not a grid of variants.
