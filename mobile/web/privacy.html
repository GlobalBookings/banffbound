Your trip and your privacy

How the BanffBound app handles your information. Updated 31 October 2026.

Trip information stays on your device

The planning feature stores your itinerary, saved places, packing checks, budget entries and booking notes in this app's local storage. You do not need an account. Device or browser backups may include this storage under your own backup settings.

Use the app's reset control to remove its saved trip data. In a browser, clearing site data also removes local plans and offline files; uninstalling a native app normally removes its app storage. Keep a personal export if you need a copy.

Sharing and exports

Sharing or exporting a trip is an action you choose. Review the contents and destination before sharing. Private booking notes are excluded from default shared summaries. An exported file is a separate copy; removing information in BanffBound does not recall copies already saved or shared elsewhere.

Location and reminders

When a feature asks for location, you can decline and continue browsing manually. Location is used to support the feature you selected. Optional reminders use your device's notification system. You can change location and notification permissions in device or browser settings.

Network requests and other services

Opening the browser app or downloading its assets sends ordinary web requests to BanffBound's hosting services. Network providers receive information needed to deliver a request, such as your IP address and technical request details.

Online maps load OpenStreetMap tile images for the area you view. OpenStreetMap receives ordinary network information, including your IP address and the requested map area. Map filters and place details run on your device. Sort nearby uses your optional location locally for straight-line distances and a position dot; it does not automatically move the map or add your GPS coordinates to external links. Weather requests go directly to Environment and Climate Change Canada (ECCC) for fixed Banff and Lake Louise station locations. Your GPS coordinates are not included in those weather requests. Opening an official report, remote image or source page sends an ordinary web request to that provider.

When you open an operator, booking, map, official source or affiliate link, that provider's privacy policy and terms apply. BanffBound may receive commission from qualifying referrals. Booking and payment are handled by the provider. Do not enter payment credentials or booking-account passwords into your trip notes.

Travel partners and affiliate links

The tours, stays and trip-extras directory includes affiliate links. BanffBound may earn a commission from qualifying bookings or purchases. These links contain our publisher and fixed campaign references; we do not add your trip notes, party details, location or a personal tracking identifier. Booking pages open in a system browser on iPhone and Android, or a new browser tab on the web. We do not receive your payment details or inspect your browsing session. The provider may use cookies under its own privacy policy and terms. Packing links lead to BanffBound's website guides, where any product affiliate links are identified separately.

Offline access

The app caches published app files and guides separately from your local trip. In the browser, completing an offline save verifies that the published app files are stored. Online maps, current information, full website articles, external pages and booking systems still need connectivity. Browser storage can be removed by your browser or operating system, especially when space is limited.

Questions and requests

Contact BanffBound with privacy questions. Include enough context to identify the issue without sending passwords or sensitive booking information. For information held by an external provider, use that provider's privacy process.

The website privacy policy separately explains optional services used on the main BanffBound website.
